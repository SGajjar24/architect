## 📐 Spatial Planning & Architectural Concept

### Architectural Vision & Massing
Corporate office complex engineered with open-span floorplates and dedicated IT infrastructure.

The architectural planning for **Spacelink** prioritizes optimal site orientation, structural efficiency, and high environmental responsiveness. The massing configuration achieves clear functional segregation, maximizes solar shading, and establishes generous cross-ventilation corridors aligned with local wind vectors in Gandhinagar.

### Vastu Shastra Harmonization
- **Entrance & Orientation**: Grand North and East facing main corporate reception entrances welcoming wealth flow
- **Functional Zoning**: Executive director cabins situated in South-West sector for authoritative decision-making
- **Element Alignment**: Finance and accounts zones placed in North sector (Kuber sthana) for financial abundance
- **Spatial Openness**: Conference & ideation rooms in North-West (Vayu) quadrant for dynamic brainstorming

## 🏢 Project Metrics & Scale

- **Vertical Envelope**: 2B/3B + Ground + 9 to 18 Floors
- **Total Capacity / Units**: 64 to 280 Offices & Retail Showrooms
- **Site Plot Area**: 2,500 – 7,000 sq. yards
- **Gross Built-Up Area**: 160,000 – 380,000 sq.ft.
- **Structural Engineering Framework**: Heavy-duty commercial RCC column-beam and shear wall core structure engineered for wide column spans (8.4m x 8.4m grid) allowing column-free internal partitioning, with IS 1893 seismic compliance.

## 📐 Floor Plans & Layout Architecture

The project features a comprehensive suite of architectural blueprints and spatial layouts:

### 1. Ground Level High-Street Retail Master Plan (`Retail Floor Plate`)
- **Spatial Scope / Area**: 12,000 – 22,000 sq.ft.
- **Layout Architecture**: Double-height retail showrooms with wide 15-to-25 ft glass display frontages, uninterrupted pedestrian promenade, separate goods loading dock, and seamless customer drop-off porch.

### 2. Typical Corporate Office Floor Plan (`Office Plate Layout`)
- **Spatial Scope / Area**: 9,500 – 18,500 sq.ft. per floor
- **Layout Architecture**: Column-free flexible office modules with 3.6-metre floor-to-floor height, central service core with AHU rooms, electrical/server risers, executive restrooms, and dual fire-escape stairwells.

### 3. Corporate Executive Suite Plan (`Unit Layout Plan`)
- **Spatial Scope / Area**: 1,200 – 2,800 sq.ft.
- **Layout Architecture**: Bespoke corporate layout featuring private director cabin with panoramic glass corner, 8-seater discussion room, open workstations bay, pantry, and private executive restroom.

### 4. Subterranean Parking & Logistics Master Plan (`Basement Services Plan`)
- **Spatial Scope / Area**: 30,000 – 65,000 sq.ft. across 2/3 levels
- **Layout Architecture**: Wide radius access ramps, automated boom barrier RFID access, fire suppression sprinkler grid, DG room, STP plant, and underground water reservoir.

## 🌿 Engineering Systems & Amenities

### Sustainable Infrastructure & MEP
- **Rainwater Harvesting**: High-capacity percolation recharge wells and subterranean filtration sumps.
- **Fire Safety & NBC Compliance**: Dual enclosed fire-escape stairwells, automatic wet-riser sprinkler systems, and smoke management shafts complying with NBC 2016.
- **Energy Conservation**: Solar PV provisions for common lighting, LED illumination, and thermal-break window systems.

### Key Amenities & Community Features
- High-speed destination-controlled passenger elevators (1.75 - 2.5 m/s)
- Double-glazed structural unitized glass facade with Low-E thermal coating
- Central VRV/VRF air-conditioning provision with energy-recovery ventilation
- 100% DG backup for essential services, elevators, and common area lighting
- Automated multi-level stack/puzzle basement parking systems
