## 📐 Spatial Planning & Architectural Concept

### Architectural Vision & Massing
Ultra-luxury high-rise residential towers on Ambli-Bopal Road with expansive glass balconies.

The architectural planning for **Viola** prioritizes optimal site orientation, structural efficiency, and high environmental responsiveness. The massing configuration achieves clear functional segregation, maximizes solar shading, and establishes generous cross-ventilation corridors aligned with local wind vectors in Ahmedabad.

### Vastu Shastra Harmonization
- **Entrance & Orientation**: East & North-East facing grand entrance foyers for positive prana flow
- **Functional Zoning**: Master suites positioned strictly in South-West (Nairutya) quadrant for grounding and stability
- **Element Alignment**: Kitchens aligned in South-East (Agni) corner with East-facing cooking platforms
- **Spatial Openness**: Spacious central living Brahmasthan kept free from heavy structural columns

## 📐 Floor Plans & Layout Architecture

The project features a comprehensive suite of architectural blueprints and spatial layouts:

### 1. Typical Floor Core & Circulation Layout (`Floor Plate Plan`)
- **Spatial Scope / Area**: 8,500 – 14,000 sq.ft. per floor
- **Layout Architecture**: Optimized 4-to-6 units per floor layout with central elevator core, dual fire escape staircases, wide 6-foot corridors, and dedicated service shafts ensuring zero cross-visibility between unit entries.

### 2. 3 BHK Master Residence Plan (`Unit Layout Plan`)
- **Spatial Scope / Area**: 1,650 – 2,250 sq.ft. Super Built-up
- **Layout Architecture**: Generous 18x12 ft living area opening into a 6-foot wide cantilevered deck, separate dining zone, master bedroom with walk-in closet & ensuite bath, secondary bedrooms with external window bays, and utility wash yard.

### 3. 2 BHK Urban Living Layout (`Unit Layout Plan`)
- **Spatial Scope / Area**: 1,180 – 1,380 sq.ft. Super Built-up
- **Layout Architecture**: Zero dead-space modular configuration maximizing carpet efficiency, open-concept living-dining, modular kitchen with attached utility balcony, and dual cross-ventilated bedrooms.

### 4. Basement & Stilt Parking Master Plan (`Parking & Services Plan`)
- **Spatial Scope / Area**: 25,000 – 50,000 sq.ft. per level
- **Layout Architecture**: Two-way 6-metre wide vehicular driveway, demarcated car bays with column protectors, EV charging points, rainwater collection sumps, and mechanical ventilation exhaust shafts.
