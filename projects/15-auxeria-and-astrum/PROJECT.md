## 📐 Spatial Planning & Architectural Concept

### Architectural Vision & Massing
Dual-tower development integrating commercial retail on lower levels with luxury residences above.

The architectural planning for **Auxeria And Astrum** prioritizes optimal site orientation, structural efficiency, and high environmental responsiveness. The massing configuration achieves clear functional segregation, maximizes solar shading, and establishes generous cross-ventilation corridors aligned with local wind vectors in Gandhinagar.

### Vastu Shastra Harmonization
- **Entrance & Orientation**: Segregated residential and commercial entry gates aligned to auspicious directional axes
- **Functional Zoning**: Commercial storefronts facing high-vibrancy North/East street frontages
- **Element Alignment**: Residential tower entrance oriented with quiet North-East landscaped gateway
- **Spatial Openness**: Kitchen and utility stacks aligned in Agni (South-East) and Vayu (North-West) quadrants

---

## 🏢 Project Metrics & Scale

- **Vertical Envelope**: 2B + Ground/Podium Retail + 12 to 16 Floors
- **Total Capacity / Units**: 90 Residential Units + 45 Commercial Outlets
- **Site Plot Area**: 3,500 – 8,000 sq. yards
- **Gross Built-Up Area**: 180,000 – 320,000 sq.ft.
- **Structural Engineering Framework**: Integrated composite structural grid featuring transfer slab/girder system at podium transition level (Level 4) transferring residential column loads into commercial grid below, fully compliant with IS 456 & IS 1893.

## 📐 Floor Plans & Layout Architecture

The project features a comprehensive suite of architectural blueprints and spatial layouts:

### 1. Ground & Podium Mixed-Use Integration Plan (`Master Podium Layout`)
- **Spatial Scope / Area**: 16,000 – 28,000 sq.ft.
- **Layout Architecture**: Street-facing retail arcades with separate vehicular arrival loops for residents and shoppers, dedicated service corridors, and security checkpoints.

### 2. Podium Amenity Deck (4th Floor) (`Amenity Landscape Plan`)
- **Spatial Scope / Area**: 12,000 – 20,000 sq.ft.
- **Layout Architecture**: Elevated residents-only podium featuring clubhouse, lap pool, kids splash pool, yoga pavilion, and landscaped gardens isolated from street noise.

### 3. Typical Residential Tower Floor Plan (`Tower Floor Plate`)
- **Spatial Scope / Area**: 8,000 – 12,500 sq.ft. per floor
- **Layout Architecture**: Four corner-facing residential residences per floor ensuring three-sided open ventilation, private foyer entries, and service elevator access.

### 4. 3 BHK Family Residence Plan (`Unit Layout Plan`)
- **Spatial Scope / Area**: 1,750 – 2,150 sq.ft. Super Built-up
- **Layout Architecture**: Spacious living room, dining bay, modular kitchen with store room & utility, three attached bathrooms, and expansive scenic balcony.

## 🌿 Engineering Systems & Amenities

### Sustainable Infrastructure & MEP
- **Rainwater Harvesting**: High-capacity percolation recharge wells and subterranean filtration sumps.
- **Fire Safety & NBC Compliance**: Dual enclosed fire-escape stairwells, automatic wet-riser sprinkler systems, and smoke management shafts complying with NBC 2016.
- **Energy Conservation**: Solar PV provisions for common lighting, LED illumination, and thermal-break window systems.

### Key Amenities & Community Features
- Complete physical segregation between commercial visitors and residential residents
- Podium-level landscaped community deck on 4th floor with swimming pool and gym
- Dedicated retail boulevard promenade with visitor surface parking
- Advanced dual-level basement car parking with access-controlled residential ramps
- Solar-powered common area illumination & organic waste composting unit
